# Privacy Policy for MapAlarm

**Effective Date: June 19, 2024**

MapAlarm ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("MapAlarm").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Location Data
To provide the core functionality of location-based alarms, MapAlarm requires access to your device's location.
*   **Background Location:** MapAlarm collects location data to trigger alarms even when the app is closed or not in use. This is essential for notifying you when you arrive at or depart from a specific destination while your screen is off or you are using other apps.
*   **Foreground Location:** Used to display your current position on the map and allow you to set alarm points precisely.

### Physical Activity
MapAlarm may use Activity Recognition to optimize battery consumption. By understanding when you are stationary, the app can reduce the frequency of GPS updates.

## 2. Data Storage
All location data, alarm settings, and search history are stored **locally on your device** using a secure Room database. We **do not** transmit your personal location data to our servers or any third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Services
MapAlarm uses Google Play Services and Google Maps API to provide mapping, search, and location functionality. These services may collect information as per their own privacy policies:
*   [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Data Security
We prioritize the security of your data. Since all data is stored locally, it is protected by the security features of the Android operating system.

## 5. Permissions
The app will explicitly request permissions for:
*   `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`: For precise proximity tracking.
*   `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`: For alerts when the app is minimized.
*   `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: To show alarm alerts and status.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings, but doing so will disable the app's core features.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
